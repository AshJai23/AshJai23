## Implication

Start with one solid retail analyst story, then demonstrate NHS reporting and
forecast validation, then engineering and evaluated retrieval. Include decisions,
quality controls, reproducible execution and limitations. A complex algorithm
without these is weaker evidence of the advertised work. Cloud familiarity can
be documented with a portable design and local implementation; never claim AWS,
Fabric, Power BI, Docker or production operation that was not actually exercised.

The data source choice is also evidence-led: UCI documents actual UK retailer
transactions and CC BY 4.0 reuse; the ONS retail index is monthly and was released
23 October with the next release listed as 20 November. Neither is a live private
retailer sales stream. NHS/public-service endpoint availability/licensing must be
verified again at implementation time; the NHS A&E landing page presented a
verification wall in this research session.
