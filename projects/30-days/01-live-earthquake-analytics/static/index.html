Local wind and storm outlook
Daily maximum wind and gusts come from Open-Meteo. Thunderstorm flags use the provider's weather codes. A high-gust flag is a separate threshold indicator. Short-term forecasts start today; the year selector applies to historical weather and yearly prediction.

Year-based prediction = monthly statistical outlook
The model learns seasonal patterns and a regularised year trend from nine earlier full years. It estimates monthly wind and high-gust-day frequency. It cannot predict exact storm dates years ahead.
Choose the target year in the left panel.
